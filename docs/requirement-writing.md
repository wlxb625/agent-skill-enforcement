# Writing requirements agents can follow

A useful requirement answers three questions:

1. What must the result do?
2. What common shortcut does not satisfy it?
3. What should the agent inspect when reviewing it?

Weak:

> The page should feel premium and cinematic.

Stronger:

> The primary composition must not be a repeated card grid. Major sections must create distinct spatial or narrative states. Dark colors, gradients, blur, and entrance fades do not by themselves satisfy this requirement.

Avoid relying only on labels such as beautiful, premium, professional, emotional, immersive, or cinematic. Explain the observable design or writing behavior behind the label.
