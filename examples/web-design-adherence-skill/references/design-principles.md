# Design principles

## Continuous composition

Build a page as a sequence of related visual states rather than a stack of interchangeable modules.

## Motion changes meaning

Useful motion changes hierarchy, context, spatial relationship, information focus, or narrative state.

## Media carries content

A photo, video, or illustration should reveal information, establish perspective, introduce the next section, or alter interpretation.

## Variable rhythm

Important sections may hold, transform, or unfold slowly. Supporting transitions may move quickly. Repeating the same timing everywhere flattens the experience.
