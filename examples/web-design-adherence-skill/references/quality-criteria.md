# Quality criteria

## Composition

- Does the page have a clear visual idea beyond a card-based layout?
- Do sections relate to one another rather than feel interchangeable?

## Scroll and motion

- Does scrolling change space, focus, hierarchy, or narrative state?
- Would the experience still be essentially identical if all motion were replaced by fade-ins? If yes, the motion system is too weak.
- Do different sections use motion appropriate to their content?

## Media

- Does each major image or video carry information or progression?
- Is text complementing the media rather than merely sitting on it?

## Responsive behavior

- Is the core experience redesigned for smaller screens rather than simply removed?
